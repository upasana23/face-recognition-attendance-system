🛡️ Biometric Privacy & Ethics

• Consenting Participants: All face images are captured with explicit user awareness and session control.

• Data Minimization: Facial images are converted to local grayscale normalized matrices and mathematical LBPH histogram vectors.

• Threshold Verification: Recognition decisions strictly enforce Chi-Square distance confidence thresholding to prevent false identity attributions.
